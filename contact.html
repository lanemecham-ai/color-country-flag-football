About
the League

Color Country Flag Football League is building a competitive, community-first flag football organization across Southern & Central Utah — starting in 2026.

Our Mission

Football For
Everyone

Color Country Flag Football League exists to grow the sport of flag football across Southern & Central Utah. We believe in competitive play, community connection, and making the game accessible to every kid in grades K–6, at every skill level.

We offer three co-ed divisions — K–1, 2–3, and 4–6 — so every player competes at the right level. Boys and girls play together in a safe, well-organized environment.

The 2026 season marks our launch. We're building something great — and we want you in it from the start.
