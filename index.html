Our Divisions

K–3

Kindergarten – 3rd Grade · Co-Ed

Where it all starts. Younger players learn the fundamentals in a fun, fast-paced setting — boys and girls on the field together.

4–6

4th – 6th Grade · Co-Ed

Bigger plays, sharper skills. Older players compete through a full fall season capped by the league championship tournament.
