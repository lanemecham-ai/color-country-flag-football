Color Country Flag Football · 2026 Season

Play for
Valley

Orange & Black

Co-ed flag football for grades K–6 is coming to Long Valley — Orderville & Glendale in 2026. Two divisions — K–3 and 4–6 — playing under official NFL FLAG rules. Register now and take the field for Valley.

What to Expect

Two Co-Ed
Divisions

K–3 and 4–6, boys and girls together. Every player competes at the right level for their grade.

NFL FLAG
Rules

Official NFL FLAG rulebook through USA Football, with background-checked coaches leading every team.

Fall
Season

Regular season games and a league championship tournament to cap it off.

Getting Started

01

Register

Sign up as a player — or register a whole team — on the league registration page.

02

Join the Valley Team

Players register with the league and play for their home community — you'll suit up alongside kids from Long Valley — Orderville & Glendale.

03

Take the Field

The fall 2026 season kicks off with regular season games and ends at the league championship tournament.

Registration fees are set by each community, with a portion going to the league to cover league-wide expenses like awards. Questions? Email colorcountryff@gmail.com.
